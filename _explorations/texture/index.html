Work, Intelligence, and Thought Science

WITS is a new research lab studying how artificial intelligence changes the way people think and work, and designing technology that strengthens human thought rather than replacing it.

Research

AI as a tool for thought

Many AI tools focus on generating content or automating processes. Though useful, these systems can affect how we think, learn, build skills, and develop expertise. We ask how AI might instead help people think better.

  • Critical thinking with AI

    Interfaces that provoke rather than replace, so that people stay engaged, reflective, and in control of their reasoning.

  • Knowledge work

    How generative AI reshapes cognition, skills, expertise, and organisations, and what that means for the future of work.

  • Programming and data

    Helping everyone program and reason with data, from spreadsheets to natural-language and AI-assisted programming.

  • Human interfaces for AI

    Design principles for intelligible, controllable AI systems, and their implications for technology, policy, and society.
