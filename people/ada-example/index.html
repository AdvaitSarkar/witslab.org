About

Ada Example is a senior affiliate of WITS Lab and a professor of computer science at Stanford University, where she leads a group studying collaboration between people and intelligent systems. This is placeholder text for a sample profile.

Her research examines how teams build shared understanding when AI tools draft, summarise, and decide on their behalf, and how interfaces can make the reasoning behind those contributions inspectable and contestable.

At WITS, she co-leads work on AI in organisations, and co-supervises doctoral students working on accountability in agent-mediated collaboration.
